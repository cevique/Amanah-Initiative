Why Amanah?

Good character doesn't stop at the screen.

We want online spaces to reflect the respect we owe one another offline. That begins with awareness, grows through everyday choices, and lasts when a community takes responsibility together.

✦

Our mission

Project Amanah is committed to fostering a culture of kindness, respect, and responsibility in the digital world. Through this initiative, we aim to raise awareness about cyberbullying and digital privacy, empowering students to build safer online communities and uphold the values of dignity, compassion, and trust in every interaction.

“The person behind the screen deserves the same respect as the person beside you.”
♡

Challenge cyberbullying

Recognise harmful behaviour, refuse to join in, and support people who are targeted. Silence can leave someone isolated; thoughtful action can help.

Values in action

Digital manners are real manners.

Our campaign connects responsible online behaviour with the everyday principles that help communities thrive.

01 / DIGNITY

Every person matters

Do not humiliate, threaten, mock, or gang up on someone online. Disagreement is not permission to harm.

02 / TRUST

Privacy is an amanah

Handle personal information with care. Get permission before forwarding messages, images, or details that belong to someone else.

03 / COMPASSION

Choose a helpful response

Pause before reacting. Report harmful content, check in with someone being targeted, and seek trusted support when needed.

Islamic perspective: Amanah means a trust or responsibility. Islamic teachings emphasise good character, guarding people's dignity, avoiding mockery and backbiting, and treating others with care. Project Amanah invites students to put these principles into practice online as well as offline.
